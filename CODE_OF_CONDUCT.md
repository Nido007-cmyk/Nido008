# Code of conduct

This project is a small, public workshop. Everyone who takes part
in it, in issues, pull requests and discussions, is expected to
keep it a place where people can work in peace.

## What we ask

- Treat people with respect, including when you disagree with
  them. Critique the code, the idea or the evidence, never the
  person.
- Assume good faith first. Most rough edges in a written thread
  are wording, not intent.
- Keep threads on the work. This repository exists to build and
  fix NIDO PC.
- Protect other people's privacy the way this project protects
  its users': never post anyone's personal data, conversations or
  files.

## What is not welcome

- Harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing private information about another person.
- Spam, advertising and off-topic promotion.
- Repeated disruption after being asked to stop.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads and,
in serious or repeated cases, block participation. Reports about
conduct can go through the same private channel as security
reports (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and are read in
confidence.

The short version: be decent, argue about the work, and leave
people's private lives out of it.
